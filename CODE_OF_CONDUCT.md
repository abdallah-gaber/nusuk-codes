# Community Code of Conduct

## Standards

Everyone should be able to participate with dignity, regardless of background, identity, ability or experience. Be considerate, welcome different perspectives, give constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual content and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

These standards apply to repository Issues, PRs and comments, and to public participation while representing the project. The owner may remove harmful contributions, restrict participation or block accounts when needed. This small, owner-maintained project has no guaranteed moderation response time.

## Reporting and enforcement

For general community concerns, open an Issue describing the concern without sensitive details. **Issues are public and are not a confidential reporting channel.** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/abdallah-gaber/nusuk-codes/issues)

For violations of GitHub policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting controls or its support contact form. Available controls depend on account permissions and repository settings. GitHub handles these reports under its own policies. [GitHub reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

**Owner follow-up required.** A dedicated private channel for sensitive Code of Conduct incidents has not yet been established. Do not publish confidential incident details in Issues. A private reporting contact must be designated before the project can offer confidential maintainer reporting.
